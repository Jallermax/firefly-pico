# Inbox

Ideas, bugs and change requests captured before implementation or a decision. Add one
top-level bullet per entry, by hand or by asking a session: `- YYYY-MM-DD area: what you saw or
want`. Continue a long entry on lines indented two spaces. A raw report needs no more detail
than it takes to recognise later. Dated Context can record checked facts and proof limits;
that context does not close or approve the request.

An entry leaves by triage: promoted to a story, a question or a feature, merged into an item
that already exists, or dropped with Maxim's yes. The rule sessions follow is the Items section
of `AGENTS.md`; the cross-project dashboard counts what is here.
